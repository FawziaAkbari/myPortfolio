01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Technology is more than code.
It’s a way to solve problems.

I build my skills by making things, and make technical ideas easier to understand by teaching them.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Kabul University’s Faculty of Computer Science. My experience includes computer teaching at Sofia High School, learner mentoring with CodeWeekend, and programming instruction at AABO.

My teaching approach is practical and step-by-step: explain the idea, demonstrate it with code, then give learners space to practice and solve problems independently.
